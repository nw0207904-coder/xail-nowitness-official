02 — ABOUT XAIL

THE BEGINNING

XAIL (ဇဲလ်) is a Myanmar independent artist, songwriter, music producer and multidisciplinary creative from Tanai, Kachin State.

The creative journey began long before the name XAIL. Life, experience, silence, failure and transformation gradually shaped the person behind the identity.

Music became the language for experiences that could not always be expressed through ordinary words. Heavy music provided intensity. Alternative music provided freedom. Different languages provided different layers of identity.

Today, XAIL's work extends beyond individual songs into visual design, creative concepts, digital creativity and multimedia projects.

XAIL is the voice.
No Witness is the philosophy.
03 — NO WITNESS

THE PHILOSOPHY

There are moments no one sees. Decisions made in silence. Failures nobody knows about. Victories that receive no applause.

No Witness represents the idea that some of the most important battles in life are ultimately faced within yourself.

It is not about hiding from the world. It is about creating without needing permission, standing without needing recognition, and continuing without needing anyone to validate the journey.

No Witness is the creative world surrounding XAIL — a space for music, ideas, identity, visual expression, experimentation and multimedia creation.

CREATE WITHOUT PERMISSION.
BUILD WITHOUT VALIDATION.
KEEP MOVING FORWARD.
